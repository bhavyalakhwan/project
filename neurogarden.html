About this garden

Why NeuroGarden?

I'm Bhavya Lakhwan — a first-year BTech CS (AI) student fascinated by the overlap between how the brain works and how we teach machines to think.

NeuroGarden is my ongoing project: a website that grows over time. The garden represents accumulating knowledge, and each new concept added is a new plant taking root.

Every week I research, simplify, and plant a new neuroscience or AI idea here — turning it into something visual, readable, and a little surprising.
